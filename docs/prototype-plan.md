# Complete prototype

The owner's request on 26 October 2026 authorizes extending the revised Living heritage direction into a complete bilingual prototype. The previous concept studies are retained separately for reference.

## Design

Forest green #173f35, deep green #10362d, ivory #f6f2e8, paper #ebe6d9 and copper #925637 remain the palette. Existing local Noto serif and sans families serve English and Bangla. The institutional name leads the homepage; the stamp remains its archival exhibit. Interior pages use an editorial title, a useful section index and a comfortable reading column. Activities and archive objects have their own source records. No new dependency or client script is needed.

The composition comes from the foundation's actual stamp, books, photographs and documents. It does not require impact counters, invented services, generic charity photographs or repeated empty cards.

## Content and verification

Use the supplied memorandum, mission statement, sign and stamp, the previously inventoried public posts and named Banglapedia articles. Attribute historical statements and organizational recollections. Do not turn founding aims into achieved outcomes. Do not publish raw private scans, signatures, identity records, personal addresses or unsupported family claims. The public document pages are clearly identified editorial summaries, not certified copies.

Unknown event dates are omitted from index metadata; the verified post date remains explicit. Detail records explain the distinction. Names, contact information and mark usage are recorded in the editorial launch notes for owner review. They do not produce repeated placeholder notices throughout the site.

Verify every static route, both language counterparts, local link and download, source timestamps, sitemap membership and exclusion of private source material. Review the rendered homepage, interior pages, activity details and sitemap at desktop and narrow mobile widths, including Bangla and keyboard navigation. The build stays a local, noindex prototype; no public deployment is part of this request.
