## Credits

The postage-stamp photograph comes from the foundation’s supplied collection. Selected activity photographs and book-cover photographs come from ISA KHAN FOUNDATION TV’s public posts. Each record links to its relevant source.

Historical introductions cite Banglapedia, the National Encyclopedia of Bangladesh published by the Asiatic Society of Bangladesh. The document reading editions are editorial summaries of the supplied institutional papers.

The website uses locally hosted Noto serif and sans-serif fonts, including Bengali fonts, under the [SIL Open Font License](/fonts/OFL.txt).

## Referencing and sharing

You can direct readers to a page by sharing its link. When citing a record, include the page title, the foundation’s name, the source named on the page and the date you accessed it. For a historical claim, consult and cite the underlying historical reference as well.

## Reproducing material

Photographs, stamp designs, book covers and third-party writing can have different rights holders. A source credit identifies where an item came from; it does not itself grant permission to republish it.

For a reproduction enquiry, send the page address, the item concerned and how you intend to use it. The foundation can respond about material it controls or help identify the relevant source. Do not assume that one permission covers all items in the collection.

## Contributing material

If you would like to contribute a photograph or document, identify its creator, date and subject as far as you can. Explain whether you own it or have permission to share it, and mention any publication restrictions. A useful description and source are as important as the image itself.
