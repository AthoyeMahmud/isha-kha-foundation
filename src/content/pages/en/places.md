## Sonargaon

Sonargaon was a political and trading centre whose history spans several periods. Its connection with Isa Khan sits within that longer story. Banglapedia distinguishes medieval Sonargaon from the surviving merchant houses of Panamnagar, which belong to a later period.

This distinction matters when reading photographs and place names: buildings in the same modern area need not belong to the same historical moment.

[Read Sonargaon in Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Sonargaon).

## Jangalbari

Jangalbari lies in Karimganj, Kishoreganj. Banglapedia discusses the fort’s association with Isa Khan and evidence that the site predates him. It treats the account of his taking possession as local tradition rather than establishing him as the fort’s original builder.

The foundation’s public visit record describes the fort area, mosque, pond and burial sites. That contemporary visit and the historical reference can be read alongside each other.

[Read Jangalbari Fort in Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Jangalbari_Fort) or [view the foundation’s visit](/en/activities/jangalbari-visit/).

## Bhati and the waterways

Bhati’s historical geography is tied to low-lying country, rivers and channels. Different writers describe its extent differently. The waterways help explain how settlements were connected and how regional rulers defended their territories.

[Read Bhati Region in Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bhati_Region).

## Looking at places carefully

The foundation’s photographs provide a record of visits and observations. They do not, on their own, date a structure or establish who built it. Historical descriptions on this website identify their source and preserve that distinction.
