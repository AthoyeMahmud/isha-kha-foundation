## An editorial reading of the memorandum

The supplied Bangla memorandum and English mission statement describe Isha Kha Foundation as a nonprofit, nonpolitical, nongovernmental voluntary and charitable organization. Its principal purpose includes research-led social, economic and humanitarian development, alongside the preservation of historical heritage.

The following thematic summary preserves the range of the objectives without presenting them as completed projects. It is a reading aid, not a verbatim or certified translation of the memorandum.

## History, heritage and culture

The opening objectives concern collecting and preserving material relating to Isa Khan, his descendants and Sonargaon. Further objectives extend that interest to the history of Bengal’s rulers, historical monuments, inscriptions, literature, folk culture, art and sport.

The memorandum also proposes educational films, research and cultural exchange. These provide context for the foundation’s documented [heritage visits](/en/activities/jangalbari-visit/), [historical discussions](/en/activities/history-discussion/) and [publications](/en/publications/).

## Education and research

The document envisages education from childhood through adulthood, scientific and technical learning, training and opportunities for employment. It refers to schools, colleges, scholarships for disadvantaged and meritorious students, and learning that includes Bangla, English, history, science, technology and cultural subjects.

Research is a recurring method throughout the text. It is applied to culture, agriculture, industry, administration, health and other areas of public life.

## Social welfare and public health

Objectives address people experiencing poverty, homelessness or exclusion, including children, older people, widows and people needing rehabilitation. Other provisions concern maternal and child health, rural medical training, awareness of disease, prevention of trafficking and drug-related harm, and support during natural disasters.

These are founding objectives. This website does not infer the existence of clinics, schools, scholarship schemes or relief operations from the document alone.

## Environment and livelihoods

The memorandum discusses tree planting, forest resources, agriculture, fisheries, animal husbandry, technical training and planned rural development. It also addresses public awareness around natural resources, industry and transport safety.

## Community and public life

The text includes harmonious relations among the people of Bengal, international cultural relationships, public awareness about rights and the rule of law, and research into institutions and administration. These are described here as parts of the founding document, without implying governmental authority or an official public service.

## Organizational scope and resources

The supplied memorandum describes an operational area across Bangladesh, with any extension abroad subject to the relevant approval. It lists contributions and donations among possible sources of funds. Those provisions do not establish that any particular fundraising facility is currently offered through this website.
