## Browsing this website

This website provides public information about the foundation. Its pages do not include visitor accounts, enquiry forms, payment processing, analytics scripts or embedded social-media players. Fonts and displayed photographs are served with the website rather than requested from a third-party font or image service.

The site does not set cookies or use browser storage for tracking. Opening a page still involves the normal connection between a browser and the server providing the page.

## Contacting the foundation

Email and telephone links let you contact the foundation through your chosen email or telephone service. Information you choose to send is part of that communication. Include only what is needed for your enquiry; a routine question about an activity or publication does not require an identity document.

For a question about information you have sent, use [isakhanfoundation@gmail.com](mailto:isakhanfoundation@gmail.com) and identify the communication concerned.

## Following external links

The website links to original YouTube posts and historical references. Opening one of those links takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. External videos do not load automatically inside these pages.

## Information in the public record

Activity descriptions identify people in their public organizational roles where relevant. The site does not distribute supplied personal identity records, private addresses, signatures or family genealogy sheets.

For a concern about a named person, photograph or document on this website, [contact the foundation](/en/contact/) with the page address and the nature of the concern.

This notice describes the current website prototype and was prepared on 26 October 2026.
