## About Isha Kha Foundation

Isha Kha Foundation brings together an interest in Bengal’s history, the legacy of Isa Khan and contemporary cultural life. Its recorded work includes heritage visits, historical discussions, book presentations, commemorative gatherings and public engagement.

The foundation was registered on **24 May 2005**. Its supplied mission statement records the name Isha Kha Foundation and registration reference **S-4732 (853)/05**. The name displayed on the supplied foundation sign also includes the alias **Isa Khan alias Isha Kha Foundation**.

## Purpose and founding aims

The memorandum describes a nonprofit, nonpolitical, nongovernmental voluntary and charitable organization. It places the collection and preservation of historical heritage alongside research, education, cultural exchange and social welfare.

These aims establish the breadth of the institution’s purpose. The activities published on this website describe particular events supported by source records. A founding aim is not, by itself, evidence that a service or project has been delivered.

## Founder and chairperson

**Nasima Sultana (Ratna)** is identified as founder and chairperson in the foundation’s public channel. The channel documents her involvement in heritage visits, discussion programmes and book presentations. The supplied publication covers also credit her writing on society and democracy.

## A record across the years

- **2005:** Registration of the foundation, followed by a heritage rally at Muktangan in Dhaka on 8 September.
- **2007:** A foundation delegation’s meeting at Bangabhaban is recorded in the public channel.
- **2009:** A discussion and prayer gathering at Baktarpur on 17 September commemorated Isa Khan.
- **2012:** The channel records an anniversary programme at the Supreme Court Bar auditorium.
- **2026:** Public posts document heritage visits, historical discussions, publication presentations and meetings about foundation programmes.

These entries follow the foundation’s own records. Each [activity page](/en/activities/) links to the relevant original post and distinguishes the event date from the post’s publication date.
