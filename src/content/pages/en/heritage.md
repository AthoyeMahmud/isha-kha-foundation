## History in its landscape

Isa Khan’s story belongs to the history of Bengal’s local rulers, river routes and shifting centres of power. This section introduces that setting through historical references and connects it with material in the foundation’s public record.

### Isa Khan and the Bara-Bhuiyans

The Bara-Bhuiyans were regional landholders and chiefs associated with resistance to Mughal expansion. Isa Khan was a leading figure in that history. Their alliances and territories changed over time; the familiar name should not be treated as a single unchanging roster.

[Read the historical introduction](/en/heritage/isa-khan/).

### Bhati: understanding the rivers

Bhati describes a low-lying river landscape. Rivers, channels and seasonal flooding shaped movement, settlement and warfare. Historical writers used the term in different ways, so it is more useful to read it in context than to place a fixed modern boundary around it.

[Explore places and the river landscape](/en/heritage/places/).

## Reading the sources

Historical reference material and a modern organization’s recollections answer different questions. The articles here name their historical references; activity pages identify what the foundation’s posts record. Local traditions are attributed as traditions rather than presented as archaeological proof.
