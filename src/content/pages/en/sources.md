## A source for each kind of record

This website brings together three kinds of material: the foundation’s supplied institutional papers, its public channel posts, and named historical reference articles. The sources serve different purposes and are identified in the relevant pages.

## Institutional papers

The supplied English mission statement and Bangla memorandum support the summaries of the foundation’s name, registration reference, institutional character and founding aims. A supplied foundation sign is the source for the alias subtitle and published contact details. The document reading editions are editorial summaries; original private scans and signatures are not distributed here.

[Read the institutional documents](/en/documents/).

## Public activity records

The public feed of [ISA KHAN FOUNDATION TV](https://www.youtube.com/@Isakhanfoundation/posts) was reviewed on **25 October 2026**. The review inventoried 99 posts. Twelve distinct activity records were selected for this website, along with publication and heritage references.

Each activity links to its original post. An account of attending a programme remains an account of participation; it does not establish that the foundation organized the programme or that another institution endorsed it. A retrospective description is attributed to the foundation’s record.

## Event dates and publication dates

An **event date** describes when an activity took place. It is used when a caption or clearly readable photograph supplies it. A **publication date** describes when the YouTube post appeared. The twelve selected posts have exact publication timestamps in their public DiscussionForumPosting metadata.

Publication times are displayed in **Dhaka time, UTC+06:00**. If an event date cannot be established, the page gives the publication timestamp without using it as the event date. A year-only event reference remains a year; no day or month is invented.

The heritage introductions draw on these named Banglapedia articles, published by the Asiatic Society of Bangladesh and consulted on **26 October 2026**:

- ABM Shamsuddin Ahmed, [Isa Khan](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php?title=Isa_Khan).
- Abdul Karim, [Bara-Bhuiyans, The](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bara-Bhuiyans,_The).
- Gazi Md. Mizanur Rahman, [Bhati Region](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bhati_Region).
- Shahnaj Husne Jahan, [Jangalbari Fort](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Jangalbari_Fort).
- [Sonargaon](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Sonargaon).

Short introductions link to the full references. Local traditions, organizational recollections and historical scholarship are distinguished in the writing.

## Photographs and publications

The stamp photograph comes from the supplied foundation collection. The visit and programme photographs and book-cover images come from the foundation’s public channel. Captions identify their context and source. A commemorative stamp is a later representation of Isa Khan, not a contemporary portrait.

Photographed covers support the visible titles and author credits. No ISBN, edition, price or publication year is assigned without a source.

## Corrections and contributions

For a correction, send the page address, the information concerned and a source that supports the change. For a photograph or document contribution, include the creator, date if known, place, context and any restrictions on publication.
